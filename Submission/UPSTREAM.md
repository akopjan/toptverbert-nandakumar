# Topology source provenance

The upstream Lean files in this directory are ported from [homotopy-groups-lean](https://github.com/Vilin97/homotopy-groups-lean), commit `c66523531ff172d7f41913d94e56921e790a1b47`, under Apache 2.0. The source root is `examples/submissions/sphere_lower_homotopy_subsingleton/Submission`. Copyright notices are preserved, including Vasily Ilin, Jiazhen Xia for the Whitehead theorem infrastructure, and the Tau Ceti attribution in the homotopy-group map and homotopy modules. The license is included here. Locally proved compatibility and generalization modules are explicitly distinguished from upstream files in the provenance manifests.

The port targets Lean 4.35.0 and pinned Mathlib `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`. Exact source hashes, port hashes, source selections, per-file compatibility changes, and the scope and results of each declaration audit are recorded in `docs/third_party/homotopy-groups-lean/`. No mathematical assumption was added. Separate manifests record the independently ported path-fibration and disk-trivialization closures.

The checked foundations include finite Kuhn interpolation preserving constant cube boundaries, ordinary based homotopy groups, relative homotopy exactness, the cube homotopy extension property, coherent singular-simplex compression, and its consequence that an `n`-connected space has zero positive-degree integral singular homology through `n`. Homology is the actual Mathlib singular homology functor. `ForMathlib/SimplexModel.lean` is a local, proved homeomorphism between the finite-coordinate simplex used by the upstream geometric constructions and Mathlib 4.35's Finsupp simplex; it commutes with all vertex maps.

`Approximation/Boundary.lean` extracts two interpolation boundary lemmas; `Homotopy/SingletonPair.lean` extracts the actual singleton-pair connectivity argument; `Hurewicz/ConnectedVanishing.lean` extracts bounded coherent compression and proves its absolute homology consequence. The project modules `ArrangementConnectivity`, `ArrangementHomology`, `QuadraticConnectivity`, and `QuadraticHomology` apply these results to the original finite linear arrangements and their actual topologies.

The subsequent port includes the full absolute Hurewicz isomorphism, its naturality, and its compatibility with the singleton relative pair. `Hurewicz/AbsoluteComparison.lean` extracts the compatibility and naturality declarations from upstream `AbsoluteNaturality.lean` to make the smaller dependency order explicit. `Hurewicz/CubicalCollapse.lean` supplies the actual relative Hurewicz isomorphism for a contractible ambient space and a sufficiently connected subspace, using the homotopy and singular-homology exact sequences. Its hypotheses are part of the mathematical theorem; it is not a general relative Hurewicz theorem for an arbitrary ambient space.

`Hurewicz/RelativeNormalization.lean` and `Hurewicz/RelativeRepresentatives.lean` are local generalizations to arbitrary pairs, proved using the ported compression and chain machinery. `Homology/SmallSimplices.lean` is a compatibility adapter to the single, independently audited Hatcher excision backend; it does not introduce another homology theory. Project modules outside this directory establish free relative classes, simplex-skeleton connectivity, the universal simplex addition relation, and the general relative vanishing argument needed by the application. Their statements use the actual topological spaces and integral singular-chain quotients.

The phase audits named `RelativeDescent`, `AbsoluteSurjectivity`, `AbsoluteIsomorphism`, and `ContractibleHurewicz` include all owned declarations, including definitions and generated private declarations. They reject newly declared axioms and permit only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` in transitive dependencies. Exact audit sources and outputs are under `verification/third_party/homotopy-groups-lean/` and `docs/third_party/homotopy-groups-lean/` respectively. The publication verification script additionally checks every vendored Lean source against its pinned provenance hash.
