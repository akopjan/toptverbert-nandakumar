# Supplementary source check for the final release

Checked on 24 November 2026. This supplements the bounded review in
`LITERATURE_REVIEW.md`; it does not establish novelty, priority, authorship,
or an exhaustive literature search.

The current publication list on
[Arseniy Akopyan's own site](https://users.mccme.ru/akopyan/papers.html)
was opened from his homepage. It contains entries through 2026, including
*Convex fair partitions into an arbitrary number of pieces*, Advances in
Mathematics 493 (2026), article 110927. No entry on the inspected list was
identified as a source for the exact ninety-vertex almost-six embedding or
the exact dimension-greater-than-seventeen obstruction in this project.
This observation concerns that one list, not every work by its author, and
does not independently establish who wrote the uploaded Lean archive.

Supplementary searches included the phrases “Akopyan convex partitions
counterexample six 17”, “Tverberg R^6 six counterexample”, “Akopyan convex
equipartition counterexample six continuous functionals dimension eighteen”,
and “topological Tverberg 2025 2026 almost 6 embedding dimension six”.
The returned exact or primary-source results did not supply an additional
published provenance for the two project constructions. Search-engine
indexing and coverage are limitations of this check.

A recent related primary record,
[*Quantifying discontinuity*, arXiv:2511.07636v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07636v3),
was inspected at the abstract and bibliographic-record level. It studies
quantitative nonembeddability and almost-embedding obstructions; its abstract
does not state the construction needed here. It was therefore not added as
a proof source. Another search result concerned topological Tverberg theorems
for products of polytopes, arXiv:2608.02979; direct retrieval of that record
failed, so no full-text review of it is claimed.

No external source has been substituted for a checked Lean proof, and no
source-author endorsement or publication status has been invented. The
release's existing mathematical-context note and metadata remain unchanged
by this supplementary search.
